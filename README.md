# cfop-selfalgs-tracker
a clean, automated google sheet template to track your cfop algs and there move count,execution time,tps and some other stats aswell.

(how to use): add your algs into the sheet and you'll see there move count, you can also add your execution time of each alg to get the Tps and the other stats.

click on "cfop selfalgs tracker" under "releases" to find the sheet file that you can download it by clicking on it.

IMPORTANT- upload the sheet to "google sheets" so it works with no issues and u can reach it on pc and mobile anytime online or offline

HAVE FUN SHARING YOUR ALGS WITH FRIENDS!
