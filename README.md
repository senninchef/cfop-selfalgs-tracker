# cfop-selfalgs-tracker
a clean, automated google sheet template to track your cfop algs and there move count,execution time,tps and some other stats aswell.

(how to use): add your algs into the sheet and you'll see there mvoe count, you can also add your execution time of each alg to get the Tps and the other stats.

click on (cfop selfalgs tracker) under releases to find the sheet file that you can download it by clicking on it.

PS: it's better to upload the sheet file to your google sheet so you can reach it on pc or mobile online or offline.
